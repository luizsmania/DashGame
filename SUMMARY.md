## 🎓 What You Learned

### PWA (Progressive Web App):
- What it is: Web app that works like native app
- How it works: Service worker + manifest
- Benefits: Offline, installable, no app store
- **This is what "Add to Home Screen" is called!**

### Player Retention:
- Daily challenges = #1 retention mechanic
- Progression systems keep players engaged
- Social features drive viral growth
- Polish makes game feel professional

### Mobile Optimization:
- Touch controls are essential
- PWA makes it feel native
- Performance matters on mobile
- One-handed play is important

## 💡 Key Takeaways

### What Works:
1. **PWA is perfect for mobile games** - No app store hassle
2. **Daily challenges drive retention** - Give players a reason to return
3. **Progression systems work** - Players love unlocking things
4. **Polish matters** - Small details make big difference
5. **Mobile-first design** - Most players are on mobile

### What to Focus On:
1. **Retention first** - Get players to return
2. **Then growth** - Share features, viral loops
3. **Then monetization** - Only after engagement is high

### Common Mistakes to Avoid:
