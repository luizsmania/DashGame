Welcome to Dash Combat!

🎮 How to Play

Movement:

Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move your ninja around the arena.

Dash Attack:

Left Click anywhere on the screen to dash to that location. Dashing into monsters will defeat them!

Objective:

Survive as long as possible and defeat as many monsters as you can. The game gets harder over time!

💡 Game Mechanics

  • Health: You start with 5 hearts. Monsters damage you when they touch you (unless you're dashing).
  • Invulnerability: After taking damage, you're briefly invulnerable (you'll flash).
  • Difficulty: Monsters spawn faster and move quicker as time passes.
  • Scoring: Each monster you defeat adds to your kill count. Beat your record!

⌨️ Controls Summary

W / ↑ - Move Up
S / ↓ - Move Down
A / ← - Move Left
D / → - Move Right
Mouse Click - Dash to cursor
R - Restart (when game over)
